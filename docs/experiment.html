03 · HOW SHARING WORKS

Zig-zag in, smooth out.

The robot fuses her brain signal, where she looks and her routine into a guess. It steers more as it gets surer, and hands control back the moment her signal disagrees.

Details for researchers

Decode. Each key press becomes a calibrated probability of "right", misreading 21% of presses, the average measured on real EEG (BCI Competition IV 2a, 9 users).

Infer. A Bayesian belief over the objects fuses that probability, gaze (with an outlier term for stray glances) and a routine prior, with forgetting so Sam can change her mind.

Share. Arm motion = α × robot direction + (1 − α) × Sam's direction. α grows with confidence up to 0.85, and drops to 0 when her signal clearly disagrees.

Measured. Right item fetched, time, share of steering kept by Sam, recovery after a change of mind, distractor grabs, and after each day: workload (NASA-TLX), felt in control, trust and enjoyment.

Real vs simulated. Real: participants, their gaze, choices, timing and ratings. Simulated: the decoder, so the brain in the loop isn't the participant's own. A live EEG study is the next step.
